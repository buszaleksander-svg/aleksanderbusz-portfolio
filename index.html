01 / MY PRODUCT

Stafflyx

Stafflyx is my own product, built to solve a real challenge in hospitality: connecting hotels with the right people for the right shifts, quickly and reliably. Hotels can post shifts, review applications and choose the professionals they prefer to work with. Hospitality workers can explore opportunities and decide which shifts to accept. This gives both hotels and workers greater choice and control.

React Native Expo iOS · Android · Web Product Development Hospitality

Developed with React Native, Expo, TypeScript and Supabase, including authentication, database integration and core workflows for workers and hotels.
